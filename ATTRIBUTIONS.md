PALPack is a small piece of hardware that only exists because of a generous open-source and maker community. The people and companies below build the platforms, firmware and content that make boards like this worth designing. Thank you.

> **A note on scope.** This repository contains **hardware only** (PCB fabrication files, a bill of materials and documentation). Listing a person or project below is a credit and a thank-you; it does not imply that they endorse PALPack or are affiliated with it. Licence details for third-party projects are noted as published in their repositories at the time of writing (September 2026), so always check upstream for the current terms.

## People & communities

### HobbySupport (@hobbysupport)

Maker and content creator in the ESP32 and hardware community.

- YouTube: <https://www.youtube.com/@hobbysupport>

### Pirata (@bmorcelli)

Developer of the **Launcher** firmware loader for ESP32 boards (M5Stack, LilyGO, Marauder and CYD devices) and a core contributor to the Bruce firmware.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/bmorcelli>
- Launcher: <https://github.com/bmorcelli/Launcher>

### The Bruce developers (BruceDevices)

The team behind **Bruce**, a versatile ESP32 firmware packed with offensive-security tools, led by @pr3y and a wide group of contributors, including Pirata (@bmorcelli) above.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/BruceDevices/firmware>
- Website: <https://bruce.computer>
- Licence: AGPL-3.0

### 7h30th3r0n3 (Evil-M5Project / Evil-Cardputer)

Author of **Evil-M5Project**, the Wi-Fi exploration and security-testing firmware for M5Stack devices, widely used on the Cardputer.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/7h30th3r0n3/Evil-M5Project>
- Licence: MIT

### LAB5 (OyczE and team), creators of MonsterC5

An independent hardware and software group focused on ESP32-C5 research and development, and the creators of **MonsterC5**, including the Cardputer ADV firmware `M5MonsterC5-CardputerADV`.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/C5Lab>
- Website: <https://lab5.eu.com>
- MonsterC5 for Cardputer ADV: <https://github.com/C5Lab/M5MonsterC5-CardputerADV>
- Licence: MIT

### The Hizmos developers (Hiktron)

The team behind **Hizmos**, an open-source ESP32-S3 pentesting, RF and STEM-education platform positioned as an alternative to the Flipper Zero.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/Hiktron/Hizmos>
- Licence: Apache-2.0

### 0ct0sec (Porkchop)

Author of **M5PORKCHOP**, the ESP32-S3 wireless-reconnaissance firmware with a playful RPG twist.

- GitHub: <https://github.com/0ct0sec/M5PORKCHOP>
- Licence: MIT
